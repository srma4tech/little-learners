Privacy Policy

For parents and guardians · Last updated: 17 October 2026

Little Learners is a child-friendly learning app. A parent or guardian should supervise its use and decide whether to enable online sync.

Information stored on this device

Learning progress, child profile names, language and voice preferences, and app settings are stored in your browser's local storage. The app may cache its static files so it can work offline. Anyone with access to this browser profile or device may be able to view that local data.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud sync

If a parent chooses Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate the parent and Cloud Firestore stores child profile names, learning progress, preferences and synchronization metadata under that parent's account. The app does not intentionally write the parent's email address or display name to the learning profile document. Google and Firebase process authentication and service data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Guest mode keeps progress on the device and does not require an account. Choosing not to merge guest progress keeps that snapshot on the device; it does not upload it as part of the account merge.

Children's privacy and consent

Parents and guardians are responsible for deciding whether to provide consent before using online account features. Do not enter a child's full legal name or other sensitive information; a short nickname is sufficient. Little Learners has no advertising or third-party analytics/tracking scripts.

Retention and deletion

You can clear local app data by clearing this site's storage in your browser. Signed-in parents can use Delete my data and account in Parent settings to request deletion of the app's Firestore profile and Firebase Authentication account. Recent authentication may be required before Firebase will delete the account. If that happens, sign in again and retry the deletion action. Deleting local browser storage is a separate action from deleting cloud data.

Security and contact

Cloud access is restricted by the Firestore security rules published for this app. No web application can guarantee absolute security. For questions or concerns, use the repository's issue tracker: Little Learners issues.
